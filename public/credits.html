THE PEOPLE AND TERMS BEHIND THE MUSIC

Credits and content.

Isai is an independent learning project with an original interface, original guided exercises, synthesized practice tones, and original abstract artwork.

Music and personal recordings

Song and composer names are factual reference metadata. The app does not bundle film recordings, lyrics, commercial scores, album artwork, or transcribed film-song lessons.

YouTube links open the official player or YouTube itself. Isai does not download or extract their audio. Playback remains subject to the uploader’s embedding settings and YouTube’s player controls.

For local transcription, use a recording and music you have the rights or permission to transcribe. Audio stays on your device; note drafts stay in your browser’s local storage. The permission confirmation does not grant rights to somebody else’s recording.

Tamil song metadata

The catalogue includes 300 factual entries selected from Tamil Film Songs Metadata, released under CC0 1.0 Universal, alongside 149 curated references. Song entries link to their metadata sources. This permission covers metadata; it does not license the film music, recordings, lyrics, or scores.

Understanding automatic drafts

Detected notes, upper-voice lines, and chord suggestions are estimates to review against your recording. They may contain extra harmonics or miss notes. Activation values are model signal strengths, not probabilities of correctness. Isai does not determine a film song’s raga or reproduce Carnatic gamakas.
